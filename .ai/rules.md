# Project Rules

## Positioning Rules

AIStaff must be positioned as the AI workforce for B2B sales conversations.

The approved primary brand promise is:

Build a business that responds, remembers, and follows through.

The approved distinctive product principle is:

AIStaff understands the sale, not just the question.

AIStaff must never be positioned as:

- A generic chatbot
- A simple FAQ bot
- A Facebook automation tool only
- A random collection of unrelated AI tools
- A Marga-affiliated service

## Purple Cow Marketing Rules

AIStaff must become remarkable through product quality.

The Purple Cow is the quality of the B2B sales conversation and the consistency of follow-through.

Marketing must show:

- Intelligent follow-up questions
- Understanding of incomplete inquiries
- Structured requirements
- Missing-information detection
- Preserved context
- Quotation readiness
- Human approval
- Clear next actions
- Consistent follow-up

Do not rely primarily on AI buzzwords, feature volume, futuristic design, or instant response claims.

The founder’s guiding philosophy is:

My marketing is my quality.

Every public claim should be supported by visible product behavior.

## B2B Sales Conversation Rules

AIStaff should treat every serious inquiry as a business opportunity that must move toward a clear next action.

AIStaff should:

1. Understand the customer’s business need
2. Ask intelligent and relevant follow-up questions
3. Avoid asking for information already provided
4. Identify missing, unclear, or conflicting requirements
5. Capture customer information in a structured form
6. Recognize buying intent, urgency, and qualification signals
7. Preserve context throughout the conversation
8. Prepare quotation-ready information
9. Recommend the appropriate next step
10. Escalate sensitive matters for human approval
11. Avoid unauthorized pricing, discounts, promises, or guarantees
12. Avoid pushing unqualified customers toward an inappropriate sale

Future agents should remain visible in public marketing to communicate the platform vision.

They must not appear purchasable or currently deployable.

## Connected Workforce Rules

The agents are connected parts of one B2B customer journey.

- AI Marketing creates the opportunity.
- AIChat handles the written sales conversation.
- AIVSA handles voice inquiries and follow-up.
- AIMA remembers meetings, decisions, commitments, and next actions.

Do not present these agents as unrelated products.

## Business Rules

AIStaff is an independent AI workforce platform.

It should provide specialized AI agents for sales, voice calls, meetings, marketing, media buying, funnel creation, social media management, customer communication, follow-ups, reporting, and operations.

The platform must remain multi-tenant, secure, local-first, and governed by role-based access and human approval.

The first public offer is AIChat Sales Agent.

## Pricing Rules

Pricing should be package-based and may include:

- Setup fees
- Monthly subscription fees
- Usage-based fees
- Add-ons
- Custom integration fees
- Project fees

Rules:

- Payment should normally be collected before setup or activation.
- Monthly plans should renew in advance.
- Setup fees may become non-refundable once implementation begins.
- Custom development requires separate quotation.
- Discounts, free trials, deferred payment, refunds, and special arrangements require owner approval.
- AIStaff must not guarantee sales, leads, ad results, revenue, or instant success.
- AIStaff may promise faster response, better organization, improved qualification, consistent follow-up, and AI-assisted execution.

## Permission and Approval Rules

The following require Owner, Super Admin, or authorized Admin approval:

1. Pricing, billing, payment, discount, refund, or subscription changes
2. Customer workspace creation, suspension, deletion, or restoration
3. User role and permission changes
4. Major AI prompt, knowledge base, script, or automation changes
5. Sensitive public customer communication
6. Campaign launches, budget changes, targeting changes, or creative approval
7. Funnel or landing page publishing
8. Data exports
9. Connecting or disconnecting integrations
10. Destructive actions or bulk changes
11. Tenant isolation exceptions
12. Production deployment or infrastructure changes
13. High-value quotations
14. Discounts and special commercial terms
15. Legal, contractual, or guarantee-related messages

AIStaff may recommend, prepare, summarize, and draft. Sensitive decisions must remain under human control.

## Data Display Rules

Users should only see data belonging to their own workspace and role.

Clients may see their own:

- Leads
- Conversations
- Follow-ups
- AI agent activity
- Qualification status
- Quotation readiness
- Campaign summaries
- Funnel performance
- Voice summaries
- Meeting actions
- Reports
- Tasks
- Subscription status
- Settings

Users must never see:

- Another customer’s data
- API keys
- Tokens
- Passwords
- Database credentials
- Webhook secrets
- Private system prompts
- Internal admin notes
- Vendor costs
- Profit margins
- Backend logs
- Database dumps
- Private infrastructure details

## Customer Communication Rules

Customer communication should be:

- Professional
- Warm
- Clear
- Consultative
- Helpful
- Business-focused
- Confident without arrogance
- Friendly without becoming overly casual

AIStaff should ask smart questions, guide customers clearly, and escalate sensitive matters.

Avoid:

- Robotic replies
- Pushy sales language
- Spammy follow-ups
- Exaggerated promises
- Unnecessary technical language
- Repeated questions
- Generic chatbot phrasing

## Security Rules

Never expose or commit:

- API keys
- Passwords
- Tokens
- Database credentials
- Webhook secrets
- Customer private data
- Production credentials
- Database backups

Secrets must not appear in GitHub, frontend code, public files, logs, or `.ai` memory files.

## Local-First Rule

The frontend, backend, PostgreSQL database, AI workflows, and internal tools should remain locally hosted unless the owner explicitly approves a different hosting direction.

Cloudflare should remain the secure public access layer rather than the primary hosting provider.

## Knowledge Sharing Rules

Reusable discoveries may be recorded in `.ai/learnings.md` under Candidate Global Skills.

Global promotion requires owner approval.

Never promote:

- Customer data
- Confidential strategy
- Private pricing arrangements
- Credentials
- Secrets
- Internal business information

## Non-Negotiables

1. Customer data isolation must never be violated.
2. Secrets must never be exposed.
3. Role-based access must never be bypassed.
4. High-risk actions require human approval.
5. AIStaff must never be presented as a Marga product.
6. AIStaff must never be positioned only as a chatbot.
7. Local-first hosting must not be changed without approval.
8. Customer communication must never overpromise.
9. Database safety must never be ignored.
10. `.ai` files must never contain confidential data.
11. AIChat must remain clearly identified as the first live product.
12. Future agents must never be presented as already available.
13. Public messaging must support the Purple Cow strategy.
