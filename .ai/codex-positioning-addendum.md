# AIStaff Codex Positioning Addendum

Before changing any public-facing AIStaff content, Codex must read:

1. `.codex-instructions.md`
2. `.ai/README.md`
3. `.ai/positioning.md`
4. `.ai/ui.md`
5. `.ai/stack.md`
6. `.ai/rules.md`
7. `.ai/roadmap.md`
8. `.ai/learnings.md`

Public-facing implementation must preserve these non-negotiables:

- AIStaff is the AI workforce for B2B sales conversations.
- The primary promise is: “Build a business that responds, remembers, and follows through.”
- The distinctive product principle is: “AIStaff understands the sale, not just the question.”
- AIChat Sales Agent is the first live product.
- AIVSA, AIMA, and AI Marketing are in development.
- Future agents must remain visible but must not appear available for purchase.
- The four agents must feel like one connected customer journey.
- Product quality must be demonstrated through realistic B2B sales conversations.
- Human approval must remain clear.
- The existing premium cinematic design must be preserved.
- AIStaff must never be presented as a chatbot or Marga product.

After implementation, update `.ai/roadmap.md` and `.ai/learnings.md`.
