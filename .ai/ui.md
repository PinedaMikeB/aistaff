# UI and UX Guidelines

## Design Goal

AIStaff should feel like a premium futuristic AI workforce platform for B2B businesses.

The interface should be cinematic, intelligent, powerful, responsive, and trustworthy. It must visually support the product promise that AIStaff understands the sale, not just the question.

The design should never feel like a generic chatbot, low-cost automation tool, playful SaaS template, or disconnected collection of AI features.

## Brand Personality

- Premium
- Cinematic
- Intelligent
- Business-focused
- Confident
- Precise
- Human-assisted
- Trustworthy
- Remarkable through quality

## Visual Style

Use a dark cinematic interface with electric blue and cyan energy, premium spacing, strong typography, glass-like depth, subtle movement, and human-AI imagery.

The visual system should communicate one connected AI workforce across B2B sales conversations, voice calls, meetings, marketing, follow-up, and operations.

## Colors

Preferred:

- Deep black
- Midnight navy
- Dark blue
- Electric blue
- Cyan glow
- Ice blue
- Soft white
- Cool gray

Blue and cyan should be used for active states, buttons, AI energy effects, glows, icons, and premium visual emphasis.

Avoid:

- Orange-heavy themes
- Yellow-heavy themes
- Red-heavy themes
- Warm gradients
- Pastels
- Rainbow gradients
- Neon green
- Pink or purple gaming palettes
- Beige or brown themes
- Cartoon-like robot colors

## Typography

Use a strong, modern, enterprise-grade typography hierarchy.

Public pages should use:

- Large, bold, high-impact hero headlines
- Short and readable supporting copy
- Strong contrast between headline, body, eyebrow, and CTA
- Controlled line length
- Generous spacing
- No weak or overly thin headings
- No crowded text blocks

The approved primary hero headline is:

Build a business that responds, remembers, and follows through.

Preserve its impact through deliberate line breaks on desktop and mobile.

## Layout Rules

Public-facing pages should be spacious, premium, cinematic, and conversion-focused.

Dashboards may be information-rich, but must use clear hierarchy, grouping, and next-action emphasis.

The website should prioritize:

1. Business transformation
2. B2B category
3. Connected workforce vision
4. Product availability
5. Product proof
6. Human control
7. Pricing
8. Sales CTA

## Spacing Rules

Use generous breathing room on marketing pages.

Avoid:

- Crowded sections
- Too many equally weighted cards
- Dense feature grids without hierarchy
- Excessive borders
- Competing CTAs
- Long paragraphs without visual breaks

## Card and Section Rules

Agent cards must communicate product status clearly.

### Live Agent Card

AIChat Sales Agent should be visually dominant and labeled:

LIVE NOW

It may use stronger glow, border, depth, or CTA treatment.

### In-Development Cards

AIVSA, AIMA, and AI Marketing should remain visible but visually secondary.

They must be labeled:

IN DEVELOPMENT

or:

COMING SOON

They must not look purchasable.

## Landing Page Rules

The homepage must communicate one connected story:

1. AIStaff is the AI workforce for B2B sales conversations.
2. AIChat Sales Agent is the first live product.
3. Voice, meeting, and marketing agents are in development.
4. All agents share one connected customer journey.
5. The quality of the sales conversation is the main product proof.

The homepage should not lead with the number of agents.

It should lead with the customer transformation:

Build a business that responds, remembers, and follows through.

The existing premium cinematic design should be preserved. Copy improvements should not trigger an unnecessary redesign.

## Product Proof Rules

The strongest visual proof should be a realistic B2B sales conversation.

The demonstration should show:

- A vague or incomplete inquiry
- Intelligent follow-up questions
- Structured requirement capture
- Missing-information detection
- Qualification status
- Quotation readiness
- Human approval
- Recommended next action

Avoid demos that only show instant FAQ replies.

## Dashboard Rules

Dashboards should help users answer:

1. What happened?
2. What needs attention?
3. What should happen next?

Prioritize:

- Leads needing attention
- Missing information
- Pending human approvals
- Overdue follow-ups
- Qualification status
- Quotation readiness
- Conversation summaries
- Recommended next actions

## Mobile Rules

Mobile layouts must preserve:

- Hero clarity
- CTA priority
- Product availability labels
- Readable conversation demos
- Clear card hierarchy
- Strong tap targets
- Premium spacing

The mobile version must not compress the page into a crowded generic SaaS layout.

## Animation Rules

Animations should be subtle, purposeful, and premium.

Approved uses:

- AI energy movement
- Soft card glow
- Controlled parallax
- Conversation progression
- Structured data appearing from a customer inquiry
- Smooth section transitions
- Status changes such as Qualified, Missing Information, or Human Approval Required

Avoid:

- Excessive movement
- Playful bouncing
- Gaming effects
- Distracting particles
- Animation that reduces readability
- Animation added only for spectacle

## Four-Agent Showcase Motion Rules

The public four-agent workforce section uses a controlled asymmetric composition rather than four equal feature cards.

- AIChat Sales Agent is the dominant visual anchor and live proof.
- AIVSA, AIMA, and AI Marketing use varied offsets, heights, and internal hierarchy while remaining in-development products.
- Card motion uses one shared cinematic grammar with role-specific choreography: AIChat uses a qualification sequence, AIVSA uses a voice signal, AIMA uses structured meeting actions, and AI Marketing uses an insight signal.
- Motion is CSS-first and supplemental. All important product descriptions, availability labels, and actions remain readable without animation.
- Hover behavior is restrained to small lift, border activation, icon response, and controlled glow.
- IntersectionObserver reveals cards when visible and removes active motion when the cards leave the viewport.
- Sticky behavior is intentionally not used for the four-agent cards to avoid trapping users or creating scroll friction.
- Mobile simplifies the asymmetry into a readable single-column sequence, removes offset behavior, keeps card-specific scenes lightweight, and preserves hierarchy.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` removes travel-heavy transitions and leaves the complete content visible.
- No new animation dependency or heavy media asset is required for the showcase.
