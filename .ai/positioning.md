# AIStaff Positioning and Messaging

## Source of Truth

This file is the primary source of truth for AIStaff positioning, marketing message, homepage hierarchy, product status, and Purple Cow strategy.

Codex must read this file before changing public-facing copy, homepage sections, service pages, pricing pages, product descriptions, CTAs, agent cards, demos, sales flows, or customer-facing onboarding.

If existing website copy conflicts with this file, Codex must preserve the existing design but align the message with this file.

## Category

AIStaff is the AI workforce for B2B sales conversations.

This category should connect the larger AI workforce vision with the first live product.

AIStaff should not be presented merely as an AI platform, chatbot, Messenger automation, or collection of agents. It should be understood as the connected AI workforce that helps B2B businesses respond, qualify, remember context, organize requirements, and follow through.

## Core Brand Promise

Build a business that responds, remembers, and follows through.

This is the approved primary homepage headline.

Do not shorten it to “Build a business that responds” unless explicitly approved by the owner.

## Core Product Statement

AIStaff understands the sale, not just the question.

This is the approved distinctive positioning line.

Use it selectively in a prominent location. Do not repeat it excessively.

## Primary Marketing Promise

Every serious inquiry receives:

1. A fast response
2. An intelligent sales conversation
3. Complete and structured requirements
4. Preserved customer context
5. A clear next action
6. Human oversight for sensitive decisions

## Purple Cow Strategy

AIStaff must become remarkable through the quality of its product experience.

The Purple Cow is not:

- Having four AI agents
- Using artificial intelligence
- A futuristic visual design
- Instant replies alone
- A long list of features
- Generic automation

The Purple Cow is:

- Asking the right question at the right time
- Understanding incomplete and messy B2B inquiries
- Identifying missing requirements
- Preserving the full sales context
- Preparing quotation-ready information
- Knowing what must happen next
- Escalating responsibly
- Following through consistently
- Making the human sales team more effective

The intended customer reaction is:

> “This is not a chatbot. This responds like a trained sales coordinator.”

Marketing must show this quality through realistic examples rather than merely claim intelligence.

## Product-Led Marketing Rule

My marketing is my quality.

Public marketing should demonstrate product behavior through:

- Realistic B2B sales conversations
- Before-and-after inquiry handling
- Structured requirement capture
- Qualification summaries
- Missing-information detection
- Quotation-readiness indicators
- Human approval points
- Conversation memory
- Recommended next actions
- Follow-up visibility

Avoid relying mainly on abstract claims, AI buzzwords, or feature volume.

## B2B Sales Conversation Standard

AIStaff should treat every serious inquiry as an opportunity that must move toward a clear next action.

A high-quality AIStaff conversation should:

1. Understand the customer’s business need
2. Ask only relevant follow-up questions
3. Avoid repeating information already provided
4. Detect unclear, missing, or conflicting details
5. Capture requirements in a structured form
6. Recognize urgency, buying intent, and qualification signals
7. Preserve context during human handoff
8. Recommend the appropriate next step
9. Avoid unauthorized promises
10. Escalate sensitive matters for human approval

## First Live Product

It handles written B2B sales conversations through Facebook Messenger and website chat.

It should be positioned as the first working proof of the larger AIStaff platform.

## Messaging to Avoid

Do not position AIStaff as:

- A chatbot
- A Facebook bot
- A simple FAQ assistant
- A generic automation agency
- A collection of unrelated tools
- A product that replaces all human judgment
- A product guaranteeing sales, leads, or revenue

Avoid generic phrases such as:

- Revolutionary
- Game-changing
- Cutting-edge
- Unlock the power of AI
- Supercharge your business
- Seamless AI solution
- Transform your business overnight

Prefer specific operational language:

- Asks the right questions
- Captures complete requirements
- Identifies missing information
- Qualifies the opportunity
- Preserves customer context
- Prepares quotation-ready details
- Recommends the next action
- Escalates for human approval
- Follows up consistently

## Brand Naming Rule

AIStaff

Do not write the official brand as:

AI Staff

The lowercase phrase “AI staff” may only be used as a generic explanatory term when it is not referring to the official brand.
