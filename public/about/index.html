About AIStaff

AIStaff builds AI sales assistant systems for businesses that receive customer inquiries through Facebook Messenger, website chat, and phone calls. Our primary service is AIChat Sales Agent, a digital subscription service that helps answer common questions, qualify leads, collect customer contact details, organize inquiries, and support follow-up workflows.

We also provide:

  • AI voice agents — AI agents that answer and place phone calls, handle inquiries, and pass qualified leads to your team.
  • Workflow automation — automated business workflows built on n8n and similar platforms, connecting the tools a business already uses so routine tasks run without manual work.
  • System integration — connecting our chat and voice agents to a client's existing systems, such as messaging channels, phone systems, spreadsheets, databases, and customer records, so information flows between them.

These services are offered as monthly subscriptions and, where a client needs custom setup work, as one-time implementation fees.
