Refund Policy

AIChat Sales Agent is a digital subscription service. Refund requests are reviewed based on the service purchased, onboarding progress, payment status, and applicable law.

  • Setup fees may become non-refundable once onboarding work begins.
  • Subscription charges may be refundable only when required by law or when AIStaff fails to deliver the purchased service.
  • Refund requests must be reviewed before approval.
  • Refund processing times depend on the original payment provider.
  • Customers may cancel future renewals before the next billing date.
  • Cancellation does not automatically refund the current billing period.

Send refund requests to support@aistaff.click with your order number, payment reference, registered email address, and reason for the request.
