Product quality in action

Closer understands the sale, not just the question.

A vague inquiry becomes a structured opportunity with missing information, qualification status, and a clear next action.

Customer Hi, we are opening a clinic in Quezon City and need five computers and two printers. Can you send a quotation?

Closer Certainly. I can help prepare the requirements for quotation. May I confirm when you need the equipment, whether you want to purchase or rent, whether the printers need color printing and scanning, and approximately how many staff will use the equipment?

Customer Rental. We need it next month. Around 20 staff. One color printer and one black-and-white printer.

Closer Thank you. I have recorded five rental computers, one color multifunction printer, one black-and-white multifunction printer, approximately 20 users, Quezon City, and target deployment next month. I still need your preferred contract period and estimated monthly print volume.

Opportunity status Qualified · requirements captured · missing information identified · quotation preparation pending · human approval required.

FAQ

Questions owners usually ask.

Is this just a chatbot?

No. It is an AI sales assistant system. It replies, qualifies leads, captures details, saves conversations, prepares quotation drafts, and helps your team follow up.

Will the AI send quotations automatically?

That is your choice. Closer can send quotations automatically the moment they are ready, or hold every one for your approval first — you pick which, and you can change it whenever. Most owners start with approval on, watch a few go out, then switch on auto-send for their standard offers once they trust it.

Can a human take over the conversation?

Yes. The system can flag conversations that need human attention, especially quotation approval, complaints, discounts, or custom requests.

Do we need a website?

No. The first version works with your Facebook Page Messenger. Website chat can be added later.

Do we need to give Facebook access?

Yes, we need to connect your Facebook Page through Meta's approved Page messaging setup so the system can receive and reply to messages.

Is there a setup fee?

No. There is no setup fee and no activation fee — the plan price is the whole price. You set Closer up yourself through a guided wizard in your dashboard that walks you through your products, prices, promos, photos, shipping and policies, one step at a time. You can skip any step and come back to it.
