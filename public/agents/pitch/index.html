FAQ

Questions owners usually ask.

Will callers know it is an AI?

If they ask, yes — Pitch is instructed to say plainly that it is an AI assistant and never to claim otherwise. Most callers work it out and carry on regardless, because they get an answer instead of a ringing phone.

Do we have to choose a language?

No, and there is no setting for it. Pitch hears how the caller speaks and replies the same way, including switching mid-call. That is deliberate: any language setting would be wrong the moment a caller mixes English and Tagalog, which is most of them.

Can it book appointments or check availability?

Not yet. Today it answers questions, captures who called and what they wanted, and can text them. It is instructed never to confirm a booking or a price as final. Booking and live availability are next on the roadmap.

How many calls can it take at once?

One line handles one caller at a time, exactly like a normal phone. If you expect overlapping calls, we add lines. We would rather tell you this upfront than have your second caller hear a busy tone.

Do we keep our number?

Pitch answers on a dedicated number we provision for you. Your existing line can be forwarded to it, so customers keep dialling the number they already know.

What happens to the call recordings?

Every call produces a transcript you can review. Recording and retention are configured with you, in line with the Data Privacy Act — we will not switch anything on without telling you.
