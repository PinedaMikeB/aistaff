FAQ

Questions brands usually ask.

How does Brandee decide what creative I need?

Brandee uses your business goal, product or service, intended audience, offer, and publishing platform to recommend the creative format, angle, hook, script, creator, voice, scenes, call to action, and number of variations.

Do I need to know how to write an ad?

No. Tell Brandee what you offer and what you want customers to do. Brandee creates an initial creative plan and script that you can approve or edit. If you already have a brief, Pro Mode lets you skip straight to production instead.

Can Brandee create static ads and videos for services?

Yes. Brandee can help create static ads and UGC videos for products and service businesses such as clinics, restaurants, real estate, professional services, events, courses, software, and local stores.

Can Brandee create creatives for messages or bookings?

Yes. Brandee can adjust the angle, script, and call to action for goals such as Messenger inquiries, appointment bookings, registrations, store visits, purchases, and retargeting.

Can I change Brandee's recommendation?

Yes. You can accept Brandee's plan or change the format, hook, script, creator, voice, scenes, call to action, platform, and variations before generating.

What do I need to get started?

A product photo, a product or service link, or even a short description of what you offer is enough for Brandee to start recommending a creative plan. A clear, well-lit product photo works best when one is available.

Can I write my own script?

Yes. Brandee suggests a script based on your product or service, but you can edit it or write your own from scratch before generating.

What languages does Brandee support?

Natural Taglish is the default for the Filipino market. Straight English or Tagalog scripts are also supported.

Do I own the videos?

Yes. Every video you generate is yours to use across your ads, social pages, and marketplaces.

How long does a video take to generate?

Most videos are ready in a few minutes, depending on length and current render load.

Will my creator remain consistent?

Brandee is designed to keep the same creator, voice, and setting across your saved videos, so customers recognize your brand from one video to the next.
