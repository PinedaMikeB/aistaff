FAQ

Questions brands usually ask.

Will my avatar look the same in every video?

Yes. Once you pick an avatar, Brandee keeps the same face and identity across every scene and every product — that consistency is the whole point.

What product photo do I need?

A clear, well-lit photo of your product on a plain or simple background works best. Brandee places it naturally into the avatar's hand.

Can I write my own script?

Yes. Brandee suggests a script based on your product info, but you can edit it or write your own from scratch before generating.

What languages does Brandee support?

Natural Taglish is the default for the Filipino market. Straight English or Tagalog scripts are also supported.

Do I own the videos?

Yes. Every video you generate is yours to use across your ads, social pages, and marketplaces.

How long does a video take to generate?

Most videos are ready in a few minutes, depending on length and current render load.
