FAQ

Questions people usually ask.

What do I need to create an ad?

A product photo and a few details — product name, description, main features, and who it's for. That's enough for Brandee to recommend a template or style and generate a preview.

Can I use a Lazada, Shopee, Facebook, or product-page link?

Yes. The product listing URL is optional. Brandee reads only that one page — never your whole website — and fills in what she can find so you can confirm or edit it.

What happens if Brandee cannot read my product link?

Brandee tells you plainly and continues using the details you entered manually. Nothing is fabricated to fill the gap.

Is the preview free?

Yes. One image preview or one 3-second video preview is free and does not require an account.

What is included in an image preview?

A low-resolution, watermarked mockup using your real uploaded product, your chosen template, and Brandee's suggested headline, copy, and CTA.

What is included in the 3-second video preview?

An actual short, watermarked motion clip representing your chosen style and concept — including the opening hook — not just a script.

Do I need to register before previewing?

No. Registration happens after you've seen your free preview, not before.

When do I need a subscription?

A subscription is required to generate the final, high-resolution, unwatermarked asset and to export it.

Will Brandee change how my product looks?

No. Brandee keeps your real uploaded product visible — packaging, shape, labels, and color are not regenerated or replaced.

Can I edit the headline or copy?

Yes. Brandee's suggested headline, copy, and CTA can be reviewed and edited before you generate a preview.

What languages are supported?

English, Filipino, and natural Taglish.

Can I generate more variations?

Yes, once you subscribe — additional variations are part of the paid plans.

What happens to my uploaded product images?

They're stored with your project so Brandee can generate your preview and, later, your final asset. They are never used for anything beyond your own ads.

Can I use the final ads commercially?

Yes. Once subscribed and exported, the final ad is yours to use across your ads, social pages, and marketplaces.
