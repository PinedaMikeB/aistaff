Delivery or Service Fulfillment Policy

AIChat Sales Agent is a digital subscription service. There is no physical delivery. Service activation begins after successful payment, completion of the onboarding form, required Facebook Page or website access, and submission of business knowledge such as FAQs, product or service information, pricing rules, and lead qualification requirements.

Standard onboarding normally begins within 1 to 3 business days after all required information and access are provided. Immediate activation is not guaranteed because setup, review, testing, and access verification may be required.

Customers are responsible for providing accurate business information and maintaining authorized access to Facebook Pages, websites, and approved integrations.
