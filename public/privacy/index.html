Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

AIStaff collects and processes information needed to provide AIChat Sales Agent, support customer onboarding, manage orders and subscriptions, and operate customer inquiry workflows.

Information we collect

  • Customer and billing details such as name, company, email, mobile number, billing address, city, province, postal code, country, tax ID, and company registration number when provided.
  • Business information such as website, Facebook Page URL, industry, estimated monthly inquiries, products or services, and onboarding preferences.
  • Messenger and website chat conversation content needed to reply to inquiries, qualify leads, save conversation history, and provide support.
  • Order, invoice, payment status, provider reference, subscription status, and support records.
  • Technical logs required for security, audit trails, webhook processing, and service reliability.

Payment data

AIStaff does not store complete card information. Card and wallet details are handled by payment providers such as Xendit or Stripe when those integrations are configured. AIStaff stores safe provider references, payment status, invoice data, and reconciliation records.

How we use information

  • Provide AI sales assistant replies, lead qualification, summaries, dashboards, onboarding, and support.
  • Create carts, orders, invoices, subscriptions, payment references, and payment status updates.
  • Send order, payment, subscription, renewal, cancellation, refund, and onboarding notifications.
  • Protect the service through validation, rate limiting, audit logs, and webhook verification.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share only the information necessary with hosting, email, analytics, payment, integration, or support providers that help deliver the service.

Retention and deletion

We keep account, order, invoice, subscription, and support records as needed for service delivery, accounting, legal compliance, dispute handling, and security. Customers may request access, correction, or deletion by emailing support@aistaff.click.

Security

AIStaff uses secure sessions, access controls, environment-based secret storage, webhook signature checks, idempotency protections, and HTTPS-only production checkout. No internet service can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email support@aistaff.click and include your company name, registered email address, and order number when relevant.
